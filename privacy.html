私隱政策使用條款English

1. 簡介

「戒色樹 QuitHK」流動應用程式（下稱「本應用程式」）由 ANH Invest（下稱「我們」）營運。本私隱政策說明我們如何處理與你有關的資料。就香港法例第 486 章《個人資料（私隱）條例》（下稱「《私隱條例》」）而言，ANH Invest 是與本應用程式有關的個人資料的資料使用者。我們致力遵守《私隱條例》及其六項保障資料原則。

簡而言之：本應用程式不設帳戶、不設我們自己的伺服器，亦不使用任何分析、廣告或追蹤工具。你在本應用程式內記錄的內容只會儲存在你自己的裝置上，我們無法查看。唯一涉及第三方的情況是訂閱付款（見第 7 節）。

2. 我們不會收集的資料

  • 本應用程式不需要註冊或登入，不會要求你提供姓名、電郵地址、電話號碼或任何身分證明資料。
  • 我們不會收集你的位置、通訊錄、相片庫、瀏覽紀錄或咪高峰錄音。
  • 我們不使用任何分析工具、當機報告工具、廣告網絡或廣告識別碼（IDFA／Android 廣告 ID）。
  • 我們不會出售、出租或分享你的資料作任何直接促銷用途。

3. 只儲存在你裝置上的資料

為提供本應用程式的功能，以下資料會儲存在你裝置的本地儲存空間（AsyncStorage；如使用網頁版，則為瀏覽器的本地儲存）：

  • 連勝紀錄：今次連勝的開始時間及最佳紀錄；植物的成長階段由此計算。
  • 重設紀錄：每次重設連勝的時間、當時的連勝長度，以及你選擇的觸發原因（如有）。
  • 每日打卡：日期、心情評分，以及你自行輸入的備註（如有）。
  • 誘惑對決：遊戲分數、植物經驗值（XP）、個人最佳、對決紀錄、每日挑戰徽章及每日已玩局數。
  • 社群互動：你讚好的帖文，以及你發佈的帖文和留言。
  • 設定及訂閱狀態：例如是否已訂閱 Pro 及所選計劃。

上述資料不會傳送給我們或任何第三方。由於部分內容（例如重設原因、心情及備註）可能屬於敏感的個人資料，我們建議你為裝置設定密碼或生物認證鎖。本應用程式不會另行為本地資料加密，其保護依賴你裝置作業系統的保安機制。

視乎你的系統設定，裝置的系統備份（例如 iCloud 或 Google 備份）可能包括本應用程式的資料。此類備份由 Apple 或 Google 按其各自的私隱政策處理，我們無法存取。

4. 緊急按鈕：鏡頭及震動

當你按下緊急按鈕時，本應用程式會令裝置震動，並在你授予相機權限後開啟前置鏡頭，於螢幕上即時顯示你自己的影像，提醒你冷靜下來。

  • 鏡頭只用作即時預覽：本應用程式不會拍照、錄影或錄音，不會儲存任何影像，亦不會將影像上載或傳送至任何地方。離開緊急按鈕畫面後，鏡頭即會關閉。
  • 本應用程式不會要求咪高峰權限。
  • 震動功能不涉及任何個人資料。
  • 相機權限屬自願性質。你可拒絕授權，或隨時在裝置的系統設定中撤回權限；屆時緊急按鈕會改為顯示文字提示，其他功能不受影響。

5. 誘惑對決

誘惑對決是一個離線小遊戲：所有情境題目均內置於本應用程式。你的作答、分數、植物經驗值、對決紀錄及每日挑戰進度只會儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳送至任何外部伺服器或第三方服務。

6. 社群

目前社群頁面顯示的帖文及留言是本應用程式內置的示範內容，並非來自其他真實用戶。你發佈的帖文、留言及讚好只會儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳送給我們或其他用戶，其他人亦無法看到。留言屬 Pro 功能。

如日後推出連線社群功能，我們會在推出前更新本私隱政策，並在有需要時徵求你的同意。

7. 訂閱及付款（第三方）

Pro 訂閱經 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 的應用程式內購買處理。付款由 Apple 或 Google 按其條款及私隱政策進行，我們不會接觸你的信用卡資料或 Apple ID／Google 帳戶資料。

我們使用 RevenueCat, Inc.（一家位於美國的公司）的服務管理訂閱狀態。RevenueCat 會收到：

  • 一個由 RevenueCat 軟件隨機產生、不包含你姓名或聯絡資料的匿名用戶 ID；
  • 購買收據及交易資料（例如所購產品、價格、貨幣、購買及續期時間）；
  • 運作所需的基本技術資料，例如裝置類型、作業系統版本、地區及貨幣設定、最後使用時間；與任何網絡連線一樣，RevenueCat 的伺服器亦會在連線時接收你的 IP 位址。

RevenueCat 不會收到你的連勝、重設、打卡、心情、誘惑對決或社群資料。上述資料只用於確認及恢復你的訂閱，並可能在香港以外地區（包括美國）處理及儲存。詳情請參閱 RevenueCat 私隱政策（revenuecat.com/privacy-policy）、Apple 私隱政策及 Google 私隱政策。

8. 資料保留及刪除

儲存在你裝置上的資料會一直保留，直至你在「設定」中選擇「清除所有本地資料」，或刪除本應用程式為止。刪除後資料無法還原，我們亦無法替你恢復。

Apple、Google 及 RevenueCat 會按其各自的政策及法律要求保留交易紀錄。如你希望刪除 RevenueCat 中與你的匿名用戶 ID 相關的紀錄，請以電郵聯絡我們；由於該 ID 屬匿名，我們可能需要你提供購買收據上的資料（例如訂單編號）以作識別。請注意，刪除本應用程式並不會取消你的訂閱（見《使用條款》）。

9. 你在《私隱條例》下的權利

根據《私隱條例》，你有權要求查閱及改正 ANH Invest（作為資料使用者）所持有關於你的個人資料。由於我們不在自己的伺服器上持有你的應用程式資料，你可直接在裝置上查看、修改或刪除大部分資料。如你就我們或我們的服務供應商可能持有的資料提出查閱或改正要求，請以電郵聯絡 ANH Invest（anhinvesthk@gmail.com），我們會在《私隱條例》規定的 40 日內回覆。我們可就處理查閱資料要求收取不超乎適度的費用。

如你對我們處理個人資料的方式有任何疑問，亦可向香港個人資料私隱專員公署查詢或投訴（www.pcpd.org.hk）。

10. 年齡限制

本應用程式只供年滿 18 歲人士使用。我們不會故意收集未滿 18 歲人士的資料。

11. 本私隱政策的修訂

我們可能不時更新本私隱政策。如有重大修訂，我們會在本應用程式內或透過應用程式更新通知你，並更新上方的生效日期。在修訂生效後繼續使用本應用程式，即表示你接受經修訂的政策。

12. 聯絡我們

本應用程式由 ANH Invest 營運。如對本私隱政策或你的個人資料有任何查詢，或欲提出查閱或改正資料要求，請電郵聯絡 ANH Invest：anhinvesthk@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

The Chinese version above is the governing version. This English version is provided for convenience.

1. Introduction

The 戒色樹 QuitHK mobile app (the "App") is operated by ANH Invest ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information about you. For the purposes of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong (the "PDPO"), ANH Invest is the data user of personal data relating to the App. We aim to comply with the PDPO and its six Data Protection Principles.

In short: the App has no accounts, no servers of our own, and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. What you record in the App stays on your own device and we cannot see it. The only third-party involvement is subscription payments (section 7).

2. What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any identity documents.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photo library, browsing history or microphone audio.
  • We use no analytics, crash reporting, ad networks or advertising identifiers (IDFA / Android Advertising ID).
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data for direct marketing.

3. Data stored only on your device

To make the App work, the following is stored in your device's local storage (AsyncStorage, or the browser's local storage on the web version): your streak start time and best streak (from which the plant stage is calculated); reset history (time, streak length and any trigger you chose); daily check-ins (date, mood rating and any note you type); your Temptation Battle scores, plant XP, personal best, battle history, daily challenge badges and rounds played today; posts you liked and posts/comments you wrote; and settings and subscription status.

This data is never sent to us or any third party. Because some of it (such as reset reasons, mood and notes) may be sensitive, we recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock. The App does not add its own encryption to local data; protection relies on your device's operating system.

Depending on your system settings, device backups (e.g. iCloud or Google backup) may include App data. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies and we cannot access them.

4. Panic button: camera and vibration

When you press the panic button, the App vibrates your device and, if you grant camera permission, opens the front camera to show a live preview of yourself on screen.

The camera is used for live preview only. The App does not take photos, record video or audio, store any image, or upload or transmit images anywhere. The camera turns off when you leave the panic screen. The App does not request microphone permission. Vibration involves no personal data. Camera permission is optional; you can refuse or revoke it at any time in system settings, and the panic screen will show a text prompt instead.

5. Temptation Battle

Temptation Battle is an offline mini-game: all scenarios are built into the App. Your answers, scores, plant XP, battle history and daily challenge progress are stored only on your device and are never sent to any external server or third party.

6. Community

Posts and comments currently shown in the Community tab are built-in sample content, not real users. Posts, comments and likes you create are stored only on your device; they are not sent to us or other users, and no one else can see them. Comments are a Pro feature. If we launch an online community in future, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where required.

7. Subscriptions and payments (third parties)

Pro subscriptions are processed as in-app purchases by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies. We never see your card details or your Apple ID / Google account information.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. (a US company) to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives: an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID that contains no name or contact details; purchase receipts and transaction data (product, price, currency, purchase and renewal times); and basic technical data such as device type, OS version, locale/currency and last-seen time. As with any network connection, RevenueCat's servers also receive your IP address when connecting.

RevenueCat does not receive your streak, resets, check-ins, mood, Temptation Battle or community data. This information is used only to verify and restore your subscription and may be processed and stored outside Hong Kong, including in the United States. See the RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy-policy), Apple and Google privacy policies.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you tap "Clear all local data" in Settings or delete the App. Deleted data cannot be recovered, and we cannot restore it for you.

Apple, Google and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their own policies and legal obligations. To request deletion of RevenueCat records linked to your anonymous ID, email us; because the ID is anonymous, we may need details from your purchase receipt (e.g. order ID) to locate it. Deleting the App does not cancel your subscription (see the Terms of Use).

9. Your rights under the PDPO

Under the PDPO you may request access to and correction of personal data held about you by ANH Invest as the data user. As we hold no App data on our own servers, you can view, edit or delete most data directly on your device. For access or correction requests regarding any data we or our service providers may hold, email ANH Invest at anhinvesthk@gmail.com and we will respond within the 40 days required by the PDPO. We may charge a fee that is not excessive for complying with a data access request. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong (www.pcpd.org.hk).

10. Age requirement

The App is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. For material changes we will notify you in the App or through an app update and revise the effective date above. Continuing to use the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

The App is operated by ANH Invest. For questions about this policy or your personal data, or to make a data access or correction request, email ANH Invest at anhinvesthk@gmail.com
